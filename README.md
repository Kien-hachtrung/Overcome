**OVERCUM**

**Overcum** is a free web application and productivity tool designed to help users quit compulsive behaviors, overcome porn addiction, and build self-discipline. Built around a sleek, dark-mode dashboard, Overcum pairs streak tracking with real-time intervention tools to foster focus and long-term habits.

*Key Features & Dashboard Overview:*

  -**Streak Counter & Goal Setting**: Displays your current progress alongside custom targets (e.g., 90-day goals) to track your daily commitment.
  -**Emergency Reset ("Need a Reset?")**: Provides immediate, judgment-free toolkits when urges strike, designed to help you regain focus within two minutes.
  -**Website Blocker & Accountability**: Monitors and blocks distracting or adult websites. Visiting restricted sites automatically resets your streak to maintain strict self-accountability.
  -**Browser Extension**: Syncs directly with your web browser to block unwanted access across the web and keep your attention aligned with your goals.
  -**Daily Motivation & Education**: Features curated inspirational insights (such as wisdom from James Clear and David Goggins) alongside dedicated educational modules.
  -**Community Integration**: Connect with peers using integrated Friends, Forum, and Leaderboard tabs to stay motivated through shared progress.
